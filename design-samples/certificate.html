Certificates and cards

Five certificate designs, each as two separate sheets: one fully Arabic, one fully English. Below them, four printable wallet cards in English. Names, dates and numbers are sample data, and every QR code is a drawn placeholder.

CERTIFICATE 1

Ivory and gold

Engraved gold border on warm ivory, the Medallion as the crest.

CERTIFICATE 2

Navy foil

Deep navy with gold lettering. Strongest on screen.

CERTIFICATE 3

Navy band

A navy band carries the Medallion and the verification code.

CERTIFICATE 4

Crest ribbon

A navy header with the Medallion set into it like a wax seal.

CERTIFICATE 5

Modern minimal

No border pattern. A large name, a fine gold rule and a faint ring.

CARD A

Navy and gold

Front and back. Standard bank-card size, 85.6 × 54 mm.

CARD B

Ivory with navy header

Matches certificates 1 and 4. Lightest on ink.

CARD C

Gold ring

The tick ring from the logo, enlarged and cropped.

CARD D

Portrait badge

Upright, for a lanyard or a site badge holder.

For printing: these show the design at card proportions. Print-ready files will need 3 mm bleed and the printer's colour profile; gold can be printed as ink or as real foil.
