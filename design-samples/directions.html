Six directions for Aman Academy

Each one is a live, animated hero built from something that belongs to safety itself, so none of them reads as a template. Switch language to see Arabic and English.

Signage

The brand speaks in the language of safety signs: square tiles, four regulated colours, hard edges, a hazard stripe.
